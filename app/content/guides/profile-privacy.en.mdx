<Sections>

<Section title="Public vs private profile" icon="user">

1. Public profile means your profile can be visited and shared.
2. Private profile reduces what others can discover from your account.
3. You can review your current privacy state directly in Account.

</Section>

<Section title="How check-ins are shown">

1. Check-ins are displayed on profile only when your profile is public.
2. If profile is private, check-in history is not publicly listed.
3. Changing profile visibility updates what visitors can see.

</Section>

<Section title="Privacy controls and safety" icon="shield">

1. Open Account settings to review profile and security options.
2. Use passkeys for stronger sign-in security when available.
3. If unsure, start private and move to public when ready.

</Section>

</Sections>
