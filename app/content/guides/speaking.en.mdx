<Sections>

<Section title="How to get started" icon="mic">

1. Open the Speak page and submit your topic proposal.
2. Describe audience value: what people will learn, solve, or rethink.
3. Add practical context so reviewers understand scope and level.

</Section>

<Section title="What makes a strong proposal">

1. Prefer focused topics over broad themes.
2. Include real examples, tradeoffs, or lessons learned.
3. Keep title and abstract specific, short, and outcome-oriented.

</Section>

<Section title="Review and next steps" icon="users">

1. Crew reviews submissions and may request clarifications.
2. Accepted talks are coordinated with format, timing, and logistics.
3. If not selected this round, you can refine and re-submit.

</Section>

</Sections>
