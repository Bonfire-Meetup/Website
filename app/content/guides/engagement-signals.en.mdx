<Surface>

<FeatureGrid>

<FeatureCard tone="rose" icon="fire" title="Sparks" badge="Spark">

Fast, lightweight reactions that help talks gain early momentum.

<Steps variant="rose-dot">

- Sparks are lightweight likes and easier to give.
- They are treated as a softer signal than boosts.
- Sparks still help recordings gain momentum.

</Steps>

<Callout tone="rose" title="Impact">

Sparks increase trending score and can help recordings surface, especially when engagement is recent.

</Callout>

</FeatureCard>

<FeatureCard tone="emerald" icon="bolt" title="Boosts" badge="Boost">

Higher-confidence votes from signed-in members.

<Steps variant="emerald-dot">

- Boosts come from signed-in accounts.
- Each account can boost a recording once, and can also spend boosts on live event questions during the open Q&A window.
- Boosts carry stronger weight in visibility ranking and help push the most relevant live questions upward.

</Steps>

<Callout tone="emerald" title="Impact">

A few boosts can noticeably increase visibility because boosts are weighted more strongly than sparks. The same idea applies to live event Q&A, where boosts help speakers notice the questions the room cares about most.

</Callout>

</FeatureCard>

</FeatureGrid>

<SectionList>

<Section title="Signal comparison">

<Steps variant="bullet">

- Sparks are easier to create and best for quick appreciation.
- Boosts are scarcer and indicate stronger intent.
- Best results usually come from a healthy mix of both signals.

</Steps>

</Section>

<Section title="Limits and allocation">

1. Boosts are limited monthly.
2. Unused boosts can accumulate only up to the configured cap.
3. Plan boosts for recordings and live event questions you want to highlight most.

<Callout>

Because boosts are limited, use them where you want to send a strong, deliberate signal.

</Callout>

</Section>

<Section title="Visibility impact">

- Recent engagement generally has stronger effect than old activity.
- A mix of sparks and boosts can improve discoverability.
- Consistent community engagement helps quality talks surface.

</Section>

</SectionList>
