<Timeline>

<TimelineStep label="Step 1" icon="log-in" title="Login or create account" href="/login" linkLabel="Open login">

Sign in first. If you are new, create your account and continue.

</TimelineStep>

<TimelineStep label="Step 2" icon="badge-check" title="Open account page" href="/me" linkLabel="Open account">

Go to your account page where your check-in tools are available.

</TimelineStep>

<TimelineStep label="Step 3" icon="badge-check" title="Click Check-in" href="/events/check-ins" linkLabel="Open check-in">

Open your check-in pass to display your personal QR code.

</TimelineStep>

<TimelineStep label="Step 4" icon="qr-code" title="Ask crew to scan it">

Show the QR code to crew at the event entrance and let them scan it.

</TimelineStep>

<TimelineStep label="Step 5" icon="search" title="QR not working? Use backup ID">

If scanning fails, tap the magnifying glass on your check-in pass to display your backup ID in large letters. Show it to crew so they can type it in manually.

</TimelineStep>

<TimelineStep label="Step 6" icon="check-circle" title="Be part of community">

Your check-ins are shown on your profile when it is public.

</TimelineStep>

</Timeline>

<Cta title="Why it matters" primaryHref="/login" primaryLabel="Login" secondaryHref="/events/check-ins" secondaryLabel="Open check-in">

When people check in, we can better understand attendance, improve planning, and celebrate the people who consistently show up for each other.

We are still deciding what exact benefits check-ins will unlock in the future. Checking in now already helps the community and event planning.

</Cta>
