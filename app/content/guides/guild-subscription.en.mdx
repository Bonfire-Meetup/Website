<Section title="Billing and management" icon="external-link">

1. You choose a Guild tier inside Bonfire, then continue to Stripe to complete checkout.
2. Payment methods, cancellations, renewals, and plan changes are managed in Stripe even after the subscription is active.
3. When you open “Manage in Stripe” from your account, you are leaving Bonfire on purpose to handle subscription changes there.

</Section>

<Section title="If account details look behind" icon="refresh">

- Most updates sync automatically after checkout or a billing change in Stripe.
- If your Guild tier or access details do not update right away, go to your account page and use the “Refresh membership” button.
- That refresh is the right first step after checkout, plan changes, or cancellations if the app still shows older subscription data.

</Section>

<Section title="When to contact support" icon="shield" size="sm">

If Stripe shows the right subscription state and Bonfire still does not reflect it after using the refresh button, contact [hello+support@bnf.events](mailto:hello+support@bnf.events).

</Section>
