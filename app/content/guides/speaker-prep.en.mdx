<TimelineStep label="Two days before" icon="mail" title="Send your slides and assets">

Send the final presentation along with any videos, music, sound effects, custom fonts, or demo files to Martin Pernica, on whatever channel you already talk to him: Discord, Messenger, email, or anything else. Tell us if your talk plays audio, if you want to present from your own device, if you want to review the video before it goes out, or if anything must stay out of the recording.

</TimelineStep>

<TimelineStep label="Event day" icon="refresh" title="Bring last-minute changes">

Still polishing? Bring a newer version on a USB-C stick, in the same format as the one you sent.

</TimelineStep>

<TimelineStep label="On stage" icon="mic" title="45 minutes, then 5 for Q&A">

The slides are ready on the stage MacBook and a presentation clicker with a built-in laser pointer is waiting for you. Talk for around 45 minutes, then take questions from the room.

</TimelineStep>

<TimelineStep label="After the event" icon="video" title="Your recording goes live">

The video is published soon after the event, unless you asked to review it first.

<FeatureCard tone="emerald" icon="check-circle" title="Do" badge="Works well">

What makes slides travel well from your laptop to our screen.

<Steps variant="emerald-dot">

- Use PowerPoint (PPTX) or Keynote.
- Build slides in 16:9.
- Embed videos and images in the file.
- Showing code? Use a large font size so it reads from the back row.
- Send custom font files along, or stick to fonts that ship with macOS.
- Go for light, bright backgrounds where your design allows.

</Steps>

</FeatureCard>

<FeatureCard tone="rose" icon="close" title="Avoid" badge="Risky">

What tends to break, or disappear, on the big screen.

<Steps variant="rose-dot">

- Live websites, streaming, or cloud data the slides depend on.
- Videos linked from the web instead of embedded.
- Dark backgrounds with thin text, which wash out on the projector.
- Switching format between the version you sent and the one on the USB-C stick.

</Steps>

</FeatureCard>

<Section title="Recording and photos" icon="camera">

<Terms>

<Term title="What we record">

You on stage, your voice, and your slides.

</Term>

<Term title="Publishing">

Recordings are published soon after the event.

</Term>

<Term title="Early access">

Some recordings are available to signed-in members or Guild members first, before they open to everyone. If you are not okay with that, let us know and we will make sure your video is open to everyone as soon as it is published.

</Term>

<Term title="Sensitive content">

If some slides or parts of the talk must not appear in the recording, such as confidential data or unreleased work, tell us beforehand and we will agree on how to handle it.

</Term>

<Term title="Photos">

You will be photographed during your talk. The photos are used on the website and in promotional materials.

</Term>

<Term title="Your portrait (optional)">

If you are comfortable with it, we would love to take a quick portrait of you at the event, before or after your talk, for the intro frame of your video.

</Term>

</Terms>

<InfoPanel icon="shield" title="Want to see the video first?">

Ask us before the event and we will wait for your approval before publishing it.
