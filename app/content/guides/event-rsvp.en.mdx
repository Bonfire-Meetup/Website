<Sections>

<Section title="How RSVP works" icon="ticket">

1. Open an event detail page from Upcoming Events.
2. Use the RSVP action and follow the selected registration platform.
3. After a successful RSVP, keep your confirmation for entry updates.

</Section>

<Section title="Where to find events">

1. Upcoming Events page lists current and next meetups.
2. Homepage events section highlights key upcoming sessions.
3. Each event detail page includes registration options and schedule info.

</Section>

<Section title="Waitlist behavior">

1. If capacity is full, the registration platform may offer a waitlist.
2. Waitlist confirmation depends on seat releases and organizer updates.
3. Stay subscribed to the event notifications so you do not miss your slot.

</Section>

<Section title="Calendar export" icon="calendar">

1. On event pages, use Add to Calendar when available.
2. You can usually choose Google Calendar or download an .ics file.
3. Adding it to your calendar helps you keep reminders and location details.

</Section>
