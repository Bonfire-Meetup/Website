# Code of Conduct

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

Bonfire is a community built on mutual respect. This Code of Conduct sets out the standard of behaviour expected of everyone who takes part in Bonfire events and community spaces, and the steps the organisers may take when that standard is not met. We are committed to a harassment-free and non-discriminatory experience for everyone, regardless of sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, age, race, ethnic origin, nationality, religion or belief.

## 1. Scope

This Code of Conduct (the “Code”) applies to all Bonfire events and spaces, whether in person or online — in particular our meetups and their accompanying programme, our Discord server, our social media channels, this website and any communication connected with Bonfire. It applies to every participant: attendees, speakers, partners and sponsors, volunteers and organisers alike.

By registering for, entering or otherwise taking part in a Bonfire event or space, you confirm that you have read this Code and undertake to follow it. The house rules of the venue and the law of the Czech Republic apply alongside this Code.

## 2. Our Values

Our events are open to anyone interested in game development. We strive to maintain a friendly and respectful environment in which professionals, students and hobbyists can meet. We ask participants to assume good intent, to be patient with newcomers and to offer constructive feedback. Everyone is expected to behave considerately and to respect the boundaries, opinions and privacy of others.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is prohibited at all Bonfire events and in all Bonfire spaces:

- **Discrimination and hate speech**: Demeaning, excluding or inciting hatred against a person or a group on the grounds of sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, age, race, ethnic origin, nationality, religion or belief.
- **Verbal harassment**: Insults, slurs, offensive remarks and unwelcome comments about a person's identity or appearance.
- **Physical harassment**: Unwanted physical contact, blocking a person's way or intruding on their personal space. Always ask before initiating physical contact.
- **Sexual harassment**: Unwelcome sexual attention or advances, sexualised comments or jokes, and the display of sexually explicit material.
- **Intimidation and stalking**: Threats, following a person without their consent, and persistent unwanted contact during or after an event.
- **Online harassment**: Any of the above carried out on our Discord server, on social media or in any other digital communication connected with Bonfire.
- **Disruption**: Sustained disruption of talks, presentations or other parts of the programme.
- **Invasion of privacy**: Photographing or recording a person in a way that intrudes on their privacy or against their express wishes, publishing private conversations, and disclosing another person's personal information without their consent.
- **Endangering safety**: Conduct that endangers the health, safety or property of others, including bringing weapons or dangerous items to an event, and failing to follow the instructions of the organisers, venue staff or emergency services.
- **Unlawful conduct**: Any conduct that breaches the law of the Czech Republic.

Encouraging or assisting any such conduct, and knowingly making a false report, are likewise prohibited.

## 4. Consequences

Anyone asked by an organiser to stop conduct that breaches this Code must comply immediately. Where a breach occurs, the organisers may take any measure they consider proportionate in the circumstances, in particular:

- a verbal warning;
- removal of content or restriction of access to an online space;
- removal from the event, without entitlement to a refund or any other compensation;
- a temporary or permanent ban from Bonfire events and spaces, including suspension or cancellation of the user account;
- notifying the venue operator, the police or another competent authority.

The organisers decide on the appropriate measure at their sole discretion, having regard to the seriousness of the conduct and the interests of those affected. Measures need not be applied in any particular order. Taking a measure is without prejudice to any claims that the injured person or the organisers may have under the applicable law.

## 5. Participation and Responsibility

Participation in Bonfire events is voluntary and at each participant's own risk. Every participant is responsible for their own conduct, for their personal belongings and for any harm they cause to other persons, to the venue or to the organisers; such harm is to be compensated under the generally applicable law, in particular Act No. 89/2012 Coll., the Civil Code.

The organisers run Bonfire as a community initiative and act to the best of their ability. To the extent permitted by law, they are not liable for the conduct of participants or other third parties, nor for the loss of or damage to items brought to an event. Nothing in this Code excludes or limits liability that cannot be excluded or limited under the applicable law, in particular liability for harm caused intentionally or through gross negligence and for harm to a person's natural rights.

## 6. Media Policy {#media-policy}

Photographs and audio-visual recordings are made at Bonfire events in order to document the events and to present and promote the community, including on this website, on our social media and in our newsletters. By attending, you acknowledge that you may appear in such material.

Where this material contains personal data, we process it on the basis of our legitimate interest in documenting and presenting our activities (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, where applicable, under the statutory licences laid down in Sections 88 and 89 of the Civil Code. Where the law requires consent — for example for a portrait used prominently for promotion — we will ask for it beforehand. Talks are recorded and published by agreement with the speaker.

You may object to being photographed or recorded at any time: tell our crew at check-in or contact us in advance, and we will make reasonable efforts to accommodate you. If you would like a particular photograph or recording in which you appear to be removed or anonymised, contact us and we will deal with your request without undue delay. Further information on the processing of personal data and on your rights is set out in the Privacy Policy below.

## 7. Nature of this Code

This Code is a set of community rules. It does not replace the applicable law, does not constitute legal advice and does not in itself give rise to any claim against the organisers to a particular procedure or outcome. The organisers may amend this Code at any time; the version published on this page at the time of the event or conduct in question applies. This Code is published in Czech and English; in the event of any discrepancy, the Czech version prevails.

## 8. Reporting and Support

If you experience or witness conduct that breaches this Code, please report it as soon as possible. Reports are handled discreetly and are shared only with those who need to know in order to deal with them, unless disclosure is required by law. You can reach us by:

- speaking directly to any member of the Bonfire crew at the event (they are clearly identifiable);
- sending us an e-mail (the inbox is monitored before, during and after events): [hello+coc@bnf.events](mailto:hello+coc@bnf.events), or use the [contact form](/contact?type=coc).

**In an emergency, or where there is an immediate threat to life, health or property, call the emergency number 112 (or the Police of the Czech Republic on 158) first.**

_We take every report seriously and will address it without undue delay. No one will face retaliation for a report made in good faith._
