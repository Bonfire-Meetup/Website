# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data are processed in connection with the website bnf.events, user accounts and Bonfire events. It is provided in accordance with Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (the General Data Protection Regulation, “GDPR”) and with Act No. 110/2019 Coll., on the Processing of Personal Data.

## 1. Data Controller

The controller of your personal data is the Bonfire organising team — an informal group of individuals who run the Bonfire community under the name Bonfire Events (“Bonfire”, “we”, “us”). Bonfire is not a separate legal entity; the organisers act jointly.

For any matter concerning personal data you can contact us at [hello@bnf.events](mailto:hello@bnf.events) or through the contact form. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as we are not required to do so.

## 2. Personal Data We Process

### 2.1 Account and sign-in data

- **Account**: e-mail address (required), optional display name, account preferences (e-mail communication, public profile), assigned roles, membership tier, and the dates of account creation and last sign-in.
- **One-time sign-in codes**: verification challenges sent to your e-mail address (the code itself is stored only as a hash), together with the IP address and browser identification (user agent) of the device that requested them.
- **Passkeys**: if you register one — the credential identifier, public key, signature counter, device type, backup state, the name you give it, and the times of creation and last use. Biometric data (such as a fingerprint or face scan) never leave your device and are not available to us.
- **Sessions**: records of issued access and refresh tokens, including the IP address and user agent.
- **Sign-in log**: sign-in attempts and their outcome and method, a hash of the e-mail address and its domain, a hash of the IP address, and a hashed and summarised user agent.

### 2.2 Community and event activity

- **Event registration and attendance**: your RSVPs and check-ins at events.
- **Questions for speakers**: questions you submit for an event Q&A and the boosts you give to questions. Questions may be displayed to other participants.
- **Recordings**: boosts you give to recordings and your Watch Later list, linked to your account. Likes (Sparks) do not require an account and are stored only as salted hashes of the IP address and a browser signature.
- **Public profile**: only if you switch it on — your display name, the date you joined, public roles and membership badge, activity statistics, boosted recordings, events attended and upcoming RSVPs become visible to anyone. The public profile is off by default and can be switched off at any time.
- **Photographs and recordings from events**: see the Media Policy in the Code of Conduct above.

### 2.3 Forms, surveys and communication

- **Contact form**: name, e-mail address, type of enquiry, subject and message.
- **Talk proposals**: speaker's name, e-mail address, talk title, abstract, duration, speaking experience and preferred location.
- **Event surveys**: the ratings and comments you submit. Survey responses are not linked to your account.
- **Newsletter**: e-mail address and the time of subscription.
- **Code of Conduct reports and other correspondence**: the content of your message and the data you choose to give us.

To protect these forms against abuse, we also store a hash of the IP address (and, for the newsletter, of the user agent) with each submission. Please do not include special categories of personal data (Article 9 GDPR) in free-text fields unless it is necessary.

### 2.4 Paid membership

If you purchase a paid membership (Bonfire Guild), the payment is processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We store the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, the membership tier, the status and billing period of the subscription, and the identifier, status, amount, currency and time of the most recent invoice and charge.

### 2.5 Technical data

- **IP address and user agent**: processed with every request in order to deliver the website, secure it and limit the rate of requests; stored in the cases described above.
- **Usage and performance data**: aggregated page views, interactions and performance metrics collected without cookies.
- **Error reports**: technical information about errors, which may include the page address, browser information and IP address.
- **Bot protection signals**: signals evaluated when you submit a form or sign in, in order to distinguish people from automated traffic.
- **Cookies and local storage**: see the Cookies and Local Storage section below.

## 3. Purposes and Legal Bases

We process personal data only for the following purposes and on the following legal bases under Article 6(1) GDPR:

- **Performance of a contract — Article 6(1)(b)**: creating and operating your account, signing you in, RSVPs and check-ins, event Q&A, boosts, the Watch Later list, paid membership, and handling your enquiries and talk proposals.
- **Consent — Article 6(1)(a)**: sending the newsletter and community e-mails, displaying your public profile, and other cases in which we expressly ask for consent. Consent may be withdrawn at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.
- **Legitimate interests — Article 6(1)(f)**: securing the service and user accounts; preventing abuse, spam and fraud (including rate limiting, bot detection and preventing duplicate likes); aggregated analytics and error diagnostics; organising and evaluating events, including surveys; documenting and presenting events through photographs and recordings; handling Code of Conduct reports; and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.
- **Legal obligation — Article 6(1)(c)**: keeping accounting and tax records relating to payments, and co-operating with public authorities where the law so requires.

Community e-mails are sent in accordance with Act No. 480/2004 Coll., on Certain Information Society Services; every such message contains a means of unsubscribing. We do not carry out automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects (Article 22 GDPR). Providing personal data is voluntary; without an e-mail address, however, we cannot create an account or reply to you.

## 4. Recipients and Processors

We do not sell personal data. We disclose them only to the following recipients and only to the extent necessary:

- **Vercel**: hosting and delivery of the website, cookieless analytics, performance monitoring and bot protection. Privacy policy: [vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Neon**: database hosting. Privacy policy: [neon.tech/legal/privacy-policy](https://neon.tech/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Resend**: delivery of e-mails (sign-in codes, notifications and newsletters). Privacy policy: [resend.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Cloudflare**: bot and spam protection (Turnstile). Privacy policy: [www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- **Rollbar**: error monitoring. Privacy policy: [rollbar.com/privacy](https://rollbar.com/privacy)
- **Stripe**: payment processing for paid membership; for part of the processing Stripe acts as an independent controller. Privacy policy: [stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy)
- **Google (YouTube)**: recordings are embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode; when you play a video, your browser connects to Google, which processes data as an independent controller. Privacy policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Discord and social networks**: our community spaces on these platforms are operated by their providers, as independent controllers, under their own terms and privacy policies.
- **Public authorities**: where we are required to do so by law.

Providers that act as our processors do so on the basis of data processing terms within the meaning of Article 28 GDPR.

## 5. Transfers Outside the EEA

Some of our providers are established in, or use sub-processors in, the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers are made on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Article 45 GDPR), where the provider is certified under it, or on the basis of the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR), together with supplementary measures where necessary. You may ask us for further information on the safeguards applied.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which they are processed:

- **Account data and activity linked to your account**: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it together with your sessions, passkeys, sign-in records linked to the account, boosts, Watch Later list, RSVPs, check-ins, event questions and membership record, as well as any newsletter subscription, contact form messages and talk proposals stored under the same e-mail address.
- **Sign-in challenges and refresh tokens**: these are valid for a limited time only (challenges for minutes, refresh tokens for up to 30 days); expired records are deleted automatically approximately 7 days after expiry.
- **Sign-in log**: for the period necessary to protect accounts and to investigate security incidents.
- **Contact form messages and talk proposals**: for the time needed to deal with them and thereafter for as long as we have a legitimate reason to keep them, or until you ask us to delete them.
- **Newsletter subscription**: until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent.
- **Likes and survey responses**: stored without direct identifiers for as long as they are needed for the operation and evaluation of the service and events.
- **Payment records**: for the period required by accounting and tax legislation.
- **Photographs and recordings from events**: for as long as they serve to document and present Bonfire's activities, or until a justified objection is upheld.

Personal data may be kept for longer where the law so requires or where this is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims, generally for the duration of the applicable limitation period.

## 7. Your Rights

Subject to the conditions laid down in the GDPR, you have the right:

- **of access (Article 15)**: to obtain confirmation as to whether we process your personal data and a copy of them; basic account data are also visible in your account;
- **to rectification (Article 16)**: to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected; some data can be changed directly in your account settings;
- **to erasure (Article 17)**: you can delete your account yourself at any time in your account settings, or ask us to erase your data;
- **to restriction of processing (Article 18)**;
- **to data portability (Article 20)**: to receive the data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format;
- **to object (Article 21)**: to object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests, including the use of photographs and recordings; you may object to direct marketing without giving any reason;
- **to withdraw consent (Article 7(3))**: at any time, for example by unsubscribing from e-mails or switching off your public profile;
- **to lodge a complaint (Article 77)**: with the supervisory authority, which in the Czech Republic is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, [www.uoou.cz](https://www.uoou.cz).

To exercise your rights, contact us at [hello@bnf.events](mailto:hello@bnf.events) or use the contact form. We will respond without undue delay and no later than one month after receiving your request; in complex cases this period may be extended by a further two months. We may ask you for the information necessary to verify your identity. Exercising your rights is free of charge, save for requests that are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

## 8. Cookies and Local Storage {#cookies}

We store information on your device only to the extent permitted by Section 89(3) of Act No. 127/2005 Coll., on Electronic Communications. We currently use only storage that is strictly necessary for the operation of the website and of the services you request, for which consent is not required. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies.

- **Strictly necessary cookies**: `bnf_csrf` (protects forms against cross-site request forgery), `bnf_refresh_token` (keeps you signed in; not accessible to scripts, valid for up to 30 days), `NEXT_LOCALE` (language preference) and `bonfire_consent` (remembers that you have dismissed the cookie notice, for 12 months).
- **Local storage in your browser**: sign-in tokens and the state of a sign-in in progress, your theme preference, the state of a membership activation, and drafts of forms you have started to fill in (contact form, talk proposal, event survey). Drafts remain in your browser until you submit the form.
- **Analytics and performance**: Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights collect aggregated data without using cookies.
- **Error monitoring**: Rollbar collects error data so that we can fix defects.
- **Bot protection**: Cloudflare Turnstile and Vercel BotID run a check in your browser when you submit a form or sign in.
- **Embedded video**: recordings are played through YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode; once you start playback, Google may store information on your device and process data under its own policies.

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings; the website may then not work properly, sign-in in particular. Should we introduce storage that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first.

## 9. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, in particular:

- passwordless sign-in using time-limited one-time codes and passkeys;
- hashing of sign-in codes and tokens, and hashing or minimisation of IP addresses and user agents wherever feasible;
- encrypted transmission (HTTPS), protection against cross-site request forgery, rate limiting and bot protection;
- access to personal data limited to organisers who need it for their role;
- ongoing maintenance and security updates.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Should a personal data breach occur that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will proceed in accordance with Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.

## 10. Children

Our online services are intended for persons aged 16 or over, and we do not knowingly process the personal data of younger children in connection with user accounts. (Under Section 7 of Act No. 110/2019 Coll., a child may consent to the processing of personal data in relation to information society services from the age of 15; we apply the higher limit of 16.) If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete them.
